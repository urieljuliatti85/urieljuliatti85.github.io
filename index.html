Safra

Production and purchasing planner for B2B processors of fresh and minimally processed vegetables that supply hospitals, restaurants and supermarkets. Instead of re-keying each week's orders across several spreadsheets, orders go in once and Safra produces the plan.

  • Weekly customer orders entered once, mapping how each customer names each product
  • Consolidates orders by product and cut and generates the daily production plan per sector
  • Calculates purchase needs at the wholesale produce market from conversion factors and stock on hand
  • Multi-tenant, built to serve many processors
  • Working prototype validated against a real week of production spreadsheets, surfacing calculation errors in the existing files
